Interpretation

What moved, and the most likely reason

Arkansas publishes outcomes, not practices. The claims below are ranked by how directly the data supports them: the numbers are fact, the attributions are inference an administrator can confirm or kill in a morning with local schedules, rosters and intervention logs.

Before you cite this

Data notes and known traps

Comparability

  • The state moved. Proficiency rose about 5 points statewide in all three subjects; F schools fell from 108 to 73. Raw year-over-year gains overstate local improvement.
  • Paired campuses share a score. P–2 buildings are graded on their 3–5 partner's tested grades. Sheridan Elementary's D and East End Elementary's C are not measurements of P–2 instruction.
  • Hold-harmless is gone. 50 schools statewide were held harmless in 2025; none in 2026, which reshapes the statewide distribution independent of teaching.
  • Growth is relative. Meeting a growth target depends on the state's expectation for that student, so a school can raise proficiency and still lose growth points.

Reporting changes

  • Low-income share jumped 9.8 points district-wide in one year (42.1% → 51.9%), and 18.3 points at East End Middle. A shift that large in a single year is far more likely an eligibility or direct-certification reporting change than a change in who lives in Grant County — verify before using it in any equity narrative.
  • Program fields were standardized. 2025 listed named programs by campus (Lexia Core5, Sonday, Take Flight, 95 Percent Group, RISE, Illustrative Mathematics, Benchmark Workshop, IXL, Jacket Time). 2026 replaced these with generic labels, so the state file can no longer tell you which intervention ran where.
  • Directory vs. accountability names differ. At East End Middle and East End Intermediate the listed principal differs from the administrator of record, consistent with a leadership move between those two buildings. Confirm locally before drawing conclusions.
  • Suppression. Groups below the state's minimum n are withheld, which is why Black and English learner cells appear and vanish between years.
