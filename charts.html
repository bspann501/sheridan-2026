Method

How these charts were made

  • Colour does one job per chart. A single blue ramp for magnitude, blue against red only where a value sits above or below a baseline, and blue against orange where two different series share a plot. No chart uses colour as its only channel — every mark carries a number, a label, or both.
  • The palette was verified, not eyeballed. Every combination was run through a contrast and colour-blindness checker for both light and dark themes, simulating protanopia and deuteranopia. The worst pair separation is well clear of the threshold in both modes.
  • Percentiles, not raw scores, wherever comparison is the point. Arkansas proficiency rose roughly five points in 2026, so raw year-over-year gains flatter every district in the state.
  • Nothing is smoothed or modelled. Every value plotted is a published figure from the state file. Where a campus shares a score with its paired P–2 partner, the charts say so rather than double-counting it.
